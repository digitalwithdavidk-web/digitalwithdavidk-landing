Day 1

Land and see The Bund

Shanghai
Morning

Land, drop your bags, grab a Luckin

Get to your hotel and test Alipay on your first Luckin coffee. If it pays, you are set for the week.

About 10 yuan with app deals
Afternoon

Yang's Dumplings, pan-fried soup buns

Crispy on the bottom, hot soup inside. Bite a small hole, sip the soup, then eat the rest. Do not chomp it whole. You will burn your mouth.

About 13 yuan for 4
Night

The Bund at night

Walk the river path. Old buildings on one side, the glowing skyline on the other. Best from about 6:30 to 9pm, when both sides are lit.

FreeLights go off earlier in winter

Tai chi and old Shanghai

Shanghai
Morning

Morning tai chi in Fuxing Park

Go early. The park fills with tai chi groups, dancers and card games. Stand in the back row and copy the moves. Nobody minds.

Go early in the morning

A garden from hundreds of years ago with rocks, ponds and curved roofs. Go in around 3pm so you are still there when the lights come on.

Day 3

Glasses, head spa, robot

Shanghai
Morning

Shanghai International Glasses City

Floors of glasses shops by Shanghai Railway Station. Pick a frame, and simple lenses can be ready in under an hour. Bring your prescription and your old glasses.

Full pairs from about 150 to 300 yuanMetro Line 4
Afternoon

Shanghai head spa

Scalp massage, warm wash and blow dry. You walk out feeling brand new. Most take about 60 to 90 minutes.

Basic about 100 to 150 yuanBook on Dianping
Night

Hotel robot room service

Order food to your hotel. In lots of Chinese hotels, a little robot rides the elevator and brings it to your door. Film it. Trust me.

Ask your hotel if they have one
Day 4

Old streets, sky high night

Shanghai
Morning

Luckin and a slow walk

Coffee first, then walk the leafy streets of the old French Concession, right by Fuxing Park. Cute cafes and old houses.

About 10 yuan coffee
Afternoon

Round 2 of soup buns

Yes, again. Then cross the river to Lujiazui, where the giant towers are.

About 13 yuan for 4
Night

Shanghai Tower, 118th floor

Go up just before sunset and stay for the city lights. Take the one flight of stairs to floor 119. Same view, way fewer people.

About 180 yuanOpen 8:30am to 10pm
Day 5

Bullet train to West Lake

Hangzhou
Morning

Shanghai Hongqiao to Hangzhou East

The bullet train takes about 45 to 60 minutes. Trains leave every few minutes. Book on Trip.com and bring your passport. It is your ticket at the gate.

About 60 to 90 yuan, second class
Afternoon

West Lake

Take Metro Line 1 to Longxiangqiao. The lake is about a 5 minute walk. Walk to the Broken Bridge and along the water. Take your time here.

Free to walkMetro about 4 to 5 yuan
Night

Sunset by the lake, train home

Watch the sun drop over the water, then ride back to Shanghai and sleep in the same hotel. No repacking.

Book your return seat early
Day 6

Gardens and canals

Suzhou
Morning

Quick train to Suzhou

About 25 to 35 minutes from Shanghai. Make sure your ticket says "Suzhou" and not "Suzhou North" so you land close to the old town.

About 30 to 45 yuan, second class
Afternoon

Humble Administrator's Garden

One of the most famous gardens in China. Book a time slot before you go with your passport number. They may not sell tickets at the gate.

About 70 yuan (about 50 in winter)Book ahead
Night

Pingjiang Road canal walk

Stone bridges, tea houses and lanterns on the water. Grab a snack, walk slow, then take the train back.

Free to walk

Before you go

5 things to set up

  1. Entry for Canadians

    Right now, Canadians can visit China for up to 30 days with no visa. That rule runs until December 31, 2026. Fill in the free arrival card online before you fly, and have at least 6 months left on your passport. Going in 2027? The rule could change. Check the official rules before you book.

  2. Pay with Alipay or WeChat Pay

    Almost everyone pays by phone. Download Alipay or WeChat Pay at home and add your Visa or Mastercard. Big payments can have a small fee (about 3% over 200 yuan on WeChat Pay). Carry a little cash just in case.

  3. Get an eSIM or a VPN

    Google, Instagram and WhatsApp are blocked in China. Buy a travel eSIM that says it works with these apps, or set up a VPN. Do it before you land.

  4. Book trains and hotels on Trip.com

    Trip.com is in English and takes foreign cards. Use your passport to book train seats. Your passport gets you through the gate.

  5. Download Luckin and Dianping

    Order Luckin coffee ahead for the best prices. Use Dianping to find and book head spas and food spots.

Prices checked October 2026. They change, so think of them as rough guides, not promises.
