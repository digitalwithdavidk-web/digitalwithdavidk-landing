      How the game works

      1. 12 missions a day from Tue Oct 27 (11 from Oct 23, 7 before). Main: post your main reel, film 1 video, make 3 UGC videos (Yoink, yaay and the next brands; record them all in one 30 to 45 min block, the Editor does the rest), send 10+ pitches (10 clears it, then go for Tier 2 and Tier 3 to fill every China day). Filming buffer (new Oct 27): keep 3+ videos filmed ahead. More posts: 1 carousel on the main page and 3+ trial reels (5 is the full target). Money: hold or book 1 call. Daily work (new Oct 23): 1 research task and 1 build task, like the Skool build. Side: gym, and lights out: off Claude by 1am, in bed by 2am.
      2. Stretch goal (not mandatory, Oct 18): 1 VIP Year client a day (US$3,997). It never counts against your 11 missions or streak.
      3. Hard work first. Side missions and easy work stay locked until the 4 main missions are done. Calls are never locked. They're money.
      4. Clear every mission for that day and you get +50 XP and a daily drop. A missed day sets the streak back to 0 and drags your success rate down.
      5. Mary habits (Oct 20): 5 lives a month. Each slip, like being inconsiderate of Mary's time, costs a life, and that day can't clear, so your streak resets. Lose all 5 before the month ends and you owe Mary something special, or a punishment she picks. Lives reset on the 1st.
      6. XP (Season 2, Oct 27): trial reel 30 each (double a main reel), main reel 15, carousel 15, UGC video 15, film 15, call held 25, call booked 10, gym 10, lights out 10, research 10, build 10, closed deal 100 each. Filming buffer: +25 a day at 3 ahead, +50 at 5. Stage clear +50. The whole history is re-scored with these weights.
      7. Pitch tiers. Tier 1: 10 pitches = 30 XP (mission done). Tier 2: 30 pitches = 70 XP. Tier 3: 50 pitches = 120 XP. Every pitch over 50 = +3 XP more. Under 10, each pitch is 1 XP.
      8. Level up from XP, to Lv 100. Each level costs more than the last (level 100 needs about 38,400 XP). Season 1 topped out at Lv 99 on Sat Oct 24, so Season 2 started Oct 27 with a steeper curve: that's Prestige 1. Only steady work every day through Jan 21 reaches Lv 100. Kade evolves every 25 levels: Street Rogue, Shadow Runner at 25, Twin Fang at 50, Frost Phantom at 75, Season Legend at 100.
      9. Filming buffer comes from the day's "buffer" number that Sterling or Sage sets (videos filmed but not posted yet). On days nobody sets it, the board carries the last number forward: plus each video filmed, minus each main reel posted.
      10. Creator rank follows the standard influencer tiers: Nano 1K to 10K, Micro 10K to 100K, Mid-tier 100K to 500K, Macro 500K to 1M, Mega 1M+.
      11. The day flips at 3am Winnipeg time. Lights out counts for the day you're finishing. Sterling checks it himself: no message from you between 1am and 3am means the mission is done.
